# Acceptance requirements

Target edition: 16 October 2026. This checklist covers the complete CONTRACT.md destination. Its clause IDs are stable. Statuses, evidence and remaining scope below mirror CONTRACT.md’s Status table. They describe the cited implementation and public-door tests; they do not certify release or hosted acceptance. Done means the bounded clause has code and public-door tests, partial means a named subset or acceptance is unmet, and missing means no implementation. New target capabilities are build requirements, not current UI or homepage promises. Buyer review round 1 added HEARTBEAT-002, PROBE-002, SCHEDULE-002, EVIDENCE-002, ROUTING-002, DELIVERY-002 and ACCESS-003 and sharpened the DIAG-001, CHAT-001, MIGRATE-001 and MIGRATE-002 journeys. Buyer review round 2 added WEBHOOK-001, PHONE-002, HISTORY-001, NOTICE-001, SLOW-001, NETWORK-002 and ARCHIVE-002 and sharpened the PHONE-001, COST-001, REPORT-001, MIGRATE-001 and MOBILE-001 journeys. Buyer review round 3 added INCIDENT-001, READAPI-001 and DEPENDENCY-002 and sharpened the FOLLOWUP-001, LAMP-001, REPORT-001, MIGRATE-001, CHAT-001 and MONITOR-001 journeys.

Local verification uses the actual migrated product Worker, signed runner, console or supported CLI, with fakes only at the network/provider edge. Use realistic US service data, authorized endpoints and dedicated synthetic accounts. Preserve existing assertions and fixtures; do not weaken checks to satisfy a clause. Prove one-off fixes with before/after cases outside the permanent suite. CI remains the full verification wall; run the checks for changed behavior locally.
